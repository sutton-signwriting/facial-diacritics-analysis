Traceable sources.

This project separates the research compilation, its software, and the contributed writing from which its records were extracted.

The selected release model retains the full current research selection, licenses the new contributions separately, and preserves applicable source terms.

Project licenses

The processing code and website implementation use the MIT license. Original research documentation and explanatory content by Steve Slevinski use CC BY 4.0. The same CC BY 4.0 grant covers publisher-created compilation and annotation contributions to the extent the publisher holds those rights.

Source writing and credit

The writing comes from SignPuddle contributors and the Sutton SignWriting community. Collection filenames, entry links, offsets, and snapshot hashes retain its provenance. The dataset compiler is not identified as the author of all source writing. Original SPML, contributor profiles, emails, free-form prose, and embedded media are outside the downloadable site; private collections are excluded.

Applicable rights and terms in source writing remain separate. SignWriting.org publishes a CC BY-SA 3.0 source notice. Its coverage and attribution for legacy contributed collections are being documented. Applicable source terms remain with the writing; the compilation license does not replace them. No exclusive claim is made over individual lexical signs or linguistic forms, and no blanket CC BY 4.0 relicensing of contributed compositions is asserted. See the machine-readable rights record; the full licensing statement accompanies the data release.

Fonts and other assets

The bundled Noto and Sutton fonts retain their supplied Noto and Sutton Open Font License notices. The rendering library retains its own license. These project licenses do not replace third-party licenses.

Citing this resource

Identify the released software version, source snapshot, analysis selection, and mapping profile. Retain source collection and entry links when citing particular examples. The local publication draft has no assigned DOI. A citable archival record will identify the exact files used; a live website address alone does not fix their version.
