Facial Diacritics Analysis: Source-linked SignPuddle Compositions and Single-Eye Gaze

This research resource provides source-linked SignWriting signboxes, normalized facial subsets, a reproducible direct mapping to official Unicode SignWriting characters, and an offline-capable static inspection website. It supports investigation of facial composition while retaining the original spatial evidence.

Contents

The primary selection contains 154,755 occurrences from 152 included source collections, with at least two retained non-S2ff facial resources. A separate selection contains 632 occurrences with positioned S321–S329 gaze resources at fill 2 or 3, without a diacritic-count threshold. Counts describe occurrences, including repeats, rather than independent words or writers.

The packages include CSVs, source witnesses and offsets, entry identifiers, source hashes and URLs, selection and mapping documentation, a data dictionary, source-context summaries, software and tests, font provenance and licenses, and a self-contained website. The source snapshot was retrieved on 1 November 2026 from the SignPuddle HTTP SPML directory. Private collections, source prose, contributor profiles, emails, embedded media, and whole SPML files are excluded from the public packages. Exact re-extraction from the whole corpus requires the matching source snapshots, which are preserved separately.

Method and interpretation

Processing uses regular expressions over existing text files. Facial subsets retain S2ff–S36c except S337–S33a, with one translation preserving relative geometry and overlap order. The direct-symbol-order-v0.1 Unicode mapping retains source symbol variants and ordering, moves the first complete S2ff resource forward or prepends default HEAD, and does not transfer coordinates.

Canonical normalization uses the archived symbol-size table; the independent live-browser metric comparison is reported separately in the verification material.

The mapping does not segment separate faces, assign marks to heads, search alternative fills, or establish reconstruction fidelity. The bundled Noto font is identified by version and checksum, while browser shaping remains environment-dependent. Display differences are not classified as font failures, encoding failures, or linguistic contrasts.

Reuse and credit

The selected release model retains the full current research selection with separate licenses for the new contributions and applicable source terms carried with the included writing.

Potential uses include inspection of authored facial relationships, evaluating conversion methods, source-linked example selection, and research on the preservation of writing. The resource is distinct from the companion manuscript's representational argument and its separately checked case selection.
