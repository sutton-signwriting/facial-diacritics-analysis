# Source licensing notice

Checked: 4 November 2026. This record documents a public notice; it does not
adjudicate every contributor's rights or license a linguistic system itself.

## Public statement

[SignWriting.org](https://www.signwriting.org/), in its licensing section near the
bottom of the homepage, offers its images and content and related Sutton Movement
Writing material under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
The page separately identifies the Open Font License for software-development
use of SignWriting symbols. The author identified the notice in this session,
and the published wording was independently checked.

## Meaning for the selected release model

The publisher selected the layered full release on 4 November 2026: retain the
current research selection, license the new contributions separately, and carry
applicable source terms with the writing. This decision does not establish
missing contributor grants or resolve the source notice's coverage by itself.

- The notice is evidence of an openly stated source policy.
- Free use of a writing system and a license over a particular authored work are
  distinct matters. No exclusive claim is made over individual linguistic forms.
- Applicable source conditions remain with included works. A CC BY 4.0 grant for
  the new compilation does not convert CC BY-SA source content to CC BY content.
- The legacy contribution flow did not consistently record explicit licensing.
  The notice's coverage of contributed collections, applicable attribution, and
  exceptions remain to be documented before public release.
- The existing research data remain intact. No field-based or collection-based
  narrowing has been applied by this website cleanup.

## Primary guidance consulted

- [U.S. Copyright Office Circular 33](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf),
  on words, short phrases, systems, and original expression; this is U.S.
  guidance, not a global determination about this dataset.
- [CC BY-SA 3.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode),
  especially sections 4(a)–4(c), distinguishing collections, adaptations, and
  attribution. Applicable ShareAlike conditions are not removed by choosing CC BY.
- [Creative Commons FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/), especially its
  discussion of included works and collection licensing, database rights, and
  rights not governed by a CC license.

This is a release-planning record, not a legal opinion or a substitute for a
documented source policy and any permissions needed for the material released.
